i System Workflow

  1. Perceive — An incident is registered by clicking the real map. A disaster has a high-intensity core and a low-intensity outer area, where an internal shelter is automatically placed.
  2. Decide — Two routes are computed: Route A to the internal shelter inside the low-intensity area, and Route B to the nearest external safe zone, avoiding the disaster core (with a detour if the direct road is blocked).
  3. Act — Both routes are rendered on the map, each with its own transport recommendation.
