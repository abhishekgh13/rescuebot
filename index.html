How RescueBot works

  1. Perceive — You click the real map of Tamil Nadu to create the disaster.
  2. Decide — RescueBot checks the nearest safe zones and picks a real road route that avoids the disaster area (it goes around it if the direct road passes through).
  3. Act — The route is drawn on the map and walk / bike / car is recommended.
