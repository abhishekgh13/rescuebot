Evacuation of People — by Disaster Area

Every disaster area gets its own route, in its own colour: the people there are taken to their nearest safe zone, plus a short route to the shelter inside the low-intensity area.

i System Workflow

  1. Perceive — A disaster is registered by clicking the real map. The affected area has a high-intensity core and a low-intensity outer area with an internal shelter.
  2. Decide — For the people of that area, RescueBot finds their nearest safe zone (from the 28 across Tamil Nadu) and a real-road route that exits their disaster and avoids every other disaster's core.
  3. Act — The evacuation route is drawn immediately, a moving marker shows the people travelling to the safe zone, and transport (walk / bike / car) is recommended for them.
