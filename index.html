i System Workflow

  1. Perceive — An incident is registered by clicking the real map.
  2. Decide — Safe zones are evaluated and a real-road route that avoids the disaster perimeter is selected. If the direct road is blocked, a detour is computed.
  3. Act — The evacuation route is rendered on the map with a transport recommendation.
